Scoring Rules / 评分规则

Overall PAVG = 0.40P + 0.10A + 0.30V + 0.20G

PASS requires unrounded PAVG ≥ 6. Missing essential inputs → no official PAVG and no official rank. Unknown ≠ 0. / 缺失必要数据不生成正式总分或排名。

Group by Creator + Platform. Sort publication dates descending and use the latest 30 valid posts. A valid post requires a valid date, nonnegative engagement and an explicit commercial flag. Unknown flags are never treated as non-commercial. Fewer than 30 valid posts withholds scoring; excluded rows are disclosed in details. Undated rows prevent an official score because their sequence is unknown. / 按达人与平台分组，使用最新30篇有效笔记；日期不明时无法确定先后，暂缓正式评分。

P — Performance · 40%

Hot Post Rate = hot posts ÷ 30. Engagement at or above the follower-band threshold qualifies as a hot post. / 互动量达到门槛即为爆文。

Preserved boundary: 1 hot post / 30 = 3.33% → P = 1; 0 hot posts → P = 0. / 保留当前边界。

V — Value · 30%

Remove one highest and one lowest engagement value from the selected 30; average the remaining 28. / 去掉一个最高值和一个最低值，计算剩余28篇的均值。

CPE = quotation ÷ trimmed mean engagement. Quotation: RMB yuan; CPE: yuan per engagement. / 报价单位人民币元，CPE单位元/次互动。

Zero trimmed mean makes CPE unavailable; V and official PAVG are withheld. 面议 or an empty quotation is missing, never zero.

G — Growth · 20%

Growth Rate = (latest 15 mean engagement − previous 15 mean engagement) ÷ previous 15 mean engagement. / 最近15篇与之前15篇按发布日期分组。

G = 70% × Growth Score + 30% × Activity Score. Round G to one decimal before weighting into PAVG, preserving the existing implementation.

Activity uses the latest post date in the Excel data as its anchor, not the computer date. Count selected posts with dates ≥ anchor − 30 days, including both boundaries. / 以该达人Excel最新笔记日为基准，包含基准日与向前第30天，仅统计选中的30篇。

If the previous 15 mean is zero, Growth Rate and Growth Score are unavailable; withhold G, official PAVG and rank. Do not substitute a growth percentage or output Infinity/NaN. / 分母为零时不虚构增长率。
